01 / OUR STORY

ESTABLISHED 2018 · DELHI NCR

Built from a simple idea: do the work properly.

S & T Enterprises was founded with a clear vision: deliver reliable, high-quality construction while building lasting relationships with clients.

Founder Mohd Kashif saw an opportunity to bring greater professionalism, attention to detail and commitment to every project. What started with a vision to build better has grown into a construction company serving clients across Delhi NCR.

Our approach is grounded in understanding requirements, communicating clearly and maintaining focus on the quality of the finished work.
